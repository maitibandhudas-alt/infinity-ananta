05 / The Bhagavad Gita

Kurukshetra, Vishvarupa and Arjuna's choice

The Mahabharata follows two branches of one family, the Pandavas and the Kauravas, as they march toward war. The Bhagavad Gita is a conversation inside it: on the field of Kurukshetra, Arjuna sees teachers and cousins in the opposing army and lowers his bow. His charioteer Krishna answers with a teaching on duty (dharma) and on acting without clinging to the result.

ॐ

Vishvarupa, the cosmic form

In chapter 11 Arjuna is given divine sight and sees all of creation within one being: countless faces, worlds beginning and ending. He is awed and shaken. The scene is an ancient picture of the limits of human understanding.
